THE ARDNILAK FOUNDATION

PLANNING

From what I was told about the site, the primary objectives were to:

  • Help bring light to good causes.
  • Donate to the organization.
  • Spread the message of the organization.

With these essentials in mind, I started the process of thumbnailing quick ideas down. I started by doing hand drawn mockups as I wanted to quickly jot down ideas to better visualize different possible structures. Using this method, I was quickly able to pinpoint which ideas I wanted to see in Figma, which took a lot less time than when I started the drafting process on the computer. It was fun to jot something down and quickly move on to the next idea.

With my favorite ideas chosen, I quickly went to Figma and began creating interactive wireframes for the client.

DRAFT 1

For the first draft, I wanted to focus on a one-page portfolio as seen in Aaron Campbell and Mauricio Ortiz’s sites. I wanted it to have a more modern look with minimal color and mostly whitespace to emphasize the graphics on the page. I wanted to keep and emphasis on his wants for the site, so I treated this as a simple solution to his problems.

DRAFT 2

For the next draft, I didn’t want to stray too far from the original design principles I used in the first. Rather, I wanted to switch the color story of the site by giving it a more graphic feel. Additionally, unlike the first draft, I wanted to separate the pages on the site to make it feel more familiar as a common webpage. I wanted the font to pop more against the color and made them wider to give it a more graphic feel.

CRITIQUE

After showing the drafts to the client, they informed me that they preferred the first draft but would like to see the donation page of the second draft incorporated, as it felt more connected with the family photo included. When drafting the final draft, I also wanted to correct the footer, as it did not adhere to Facebook’s brand guidelines for using their logo.

CODING

Using Sublime Text, I edit the site using HTML and CSS. This project allowed me to flex new muscles such as learning to:

  • Nest columns inside of each other.
  • Make an interactive contact form with the help of FormSubmit.
  • Make containers more flexible for easier mobile viewing.

With what I learned in my first project, I was more through when coding to shrink my window to show if my assets were responsive. Additionally, taking a more responsive design approach made the need for media inquiries very limited. I am very happy with the design of this website, as I could see an improvement in my abilities as a coder. I bring what I have learned to every project moving forward, so I cannot wait to see what other designs I can create.
